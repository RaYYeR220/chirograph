The Story of Chirograph02 / 06

Bitcoin Cash got its dollar. It can't get out.

Most people arrive in crypto through stablecoins, and almost all of them sit on EVM chains. ParyonUSD gave Bitcoin Cash its own dollar this year: over $1M locked, and the peg held while BCH fell 37%. But no bridge moves a CashToken off BCH, so dollars come in and out through an exchange or a slow multi-hop route.

So we cut a deed in two.

A medieval chirograph was one deed written twice and cut apart through a word; only the two halves match. Chirograph locks USDC on its chain and PUSD on Bitcoin Cash to the same 32-byte secret. Revealing it to collect one side is exactly what lets the other side collect theirs. Nothing is bridged. Only the secret crosses.

A Base shopper pays a Bitcoin Cash merchant with USDC; the merchant's Paytaca shows PUSD seconds later.

BCH Usage04 / 06

A 72-byte keyless covenant.

  • CashScript 0.14, P2SH32. claim(secret) / refund(), no signatures at all.
  • Introspection pins the payout: destination, token category, amount, NFT commitment, fee ≤ 1,000 sats.
  • Shared payout rule compiled into OP_DEFINE / OP_INVOKE from the May 2026 upgrade.
  • Anyone can execute a claim or refund; nobody can redirect it.

BCH's speed, used honestly.

  • PUSD and MUSD (CashTokens) through the same covenant; WizardConnect connects Selene, Paytaca, Cashonize.
  • 0-conf + double-spend proofs: 4 s window, P2PKH-only inputs, capped per swap and in total.
  • Every swap is real BCH mainnet transactions a judge makes from the live site.
  • One-minute blocks (locked in Nov 15) shrink every timeout from May 2027.
20 covenant tests · 64 EVM tests incl. fork tests on real USDC · end-to-end through the real WizardConnect protocol
The Vision for Chirograph05 / 06

The on-ramp BCH dollars need.

  • More pairs: USDT, Solana, Tron; PUSD V2 and any other CashToken stable (MUSD already works).

How it makes money.

  • Spread on every swap (0.3% today) for solvers.
  • Widget fee for merchant checkout volume.
  • Routing: Chirograph for speed under the 0-conf cap, THORChain for size.

Next priority: a second solver and native USDC liquidity on BCH, without a single custodian.
